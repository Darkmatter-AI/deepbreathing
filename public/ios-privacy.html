Deep Breathing for iOS: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026.

This policy applies to the Deep Breathing: Calm & Sleep iOS app, version 1.0.1 and later.

Guest practice

You can use the breathing exercises without an account. Guest practice history, totals, and settings stay on your device until you choose to sign in.

Optional accounts and practice sync

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your account identifier, email address, and any name the provider shares. We store your practice history, totals, streak days, and settings to sync your practice between your phone and the website.

No usage analytics or tracking

The iOS app does not collect usage analytics, create analytics identifiers, or send analytics events to Google Analytics. The app removes any analytics identifier and analytics preference saved by an earlier version.

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use the advertising identifier, combine your app data with third-party data for advertising, share your data with data brokers, or sell your personal information.

How we use and share account data

We use account and synced practice data for authentication, practice sync, account management, and support. Service providers help us run authentication, email, hosting, and database storage. Apple or Google handles sign-in when you choose that provider. We do not use breathing data for advertising.

Device features

Audio and haptics play on your device. The app does not use the microphone, HealthKit, precise location, contacts, or photos.

Your choices and deletion

Sign-in is optional. To delete your account, open the account menu and choose Delete account. Confirm deletion through the email link we send. After confirmation, we delete the account and synced practice data. Delete the app to remove local practice data.

Websites and external links

The website and external pages have their own privacy practices. The website privacy policy describes website data use.
