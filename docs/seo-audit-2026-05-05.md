## TL;DR

1. **🚨 Impressions collapsed ~50% on BOTH Google and Bing simultaneously between March and April–May.**
   - Google: 293 → 137 clicks, 54,959 → 25,649 imp, both **−53%**
   - Bing: 265 → 126 clicks (-52%), 8,130 → 4,590 imp (-44%)
   - Position and CTR barely moved on either engine.
   - **The cause is almost certainly the April 1 sitemap conversion** — commits `c6826f2` (Add hreflang alternates to sitemap) and `361994d` (Convert sitemap to route handler for xhtml:link hreflang support) landed on **2026-04-01**, which lines up exactly with the impression cliff (mid-March was 1,400–2,500 imp/day; end-March crashed to 600–800/day).
   - **This was already known and remediation is in progress**: the April 20 commit cluster (`b7bb201`, `ef876fe`, `072ab3a`, `1a06ede`, `aa3ff55`, `b5e850f`, `e2723f9`, `092a1aa`) fixed SSR language-switcher issues with the MT proxy, added a `/languages` discovery page, and bulk-resubmitted 110 URLs to GSC + 126 to Bing. Today's commit `7a231b2` reverted `/languages` noindex now that "MT proxy now excludes /languages directly".
   - **Per `docs/indexing-queue.md` (2026-04-20)**: 307 URLs in sitemap, only 180 indexed by Google — **41% of pages were de-indexed** during the April incident. The recovery is mid-flight: many resubmitted URLs are still pending re-indexing.
2. **Bing is quietly delivering more traffic than Google.** 525 Bing clicks vs 478 Google clicks (90d). Bing CTR is 3.28% (6× Google's 0.53%) and avg position is 4.95 (7 positions better than Google). Bing is the bigger win we've been undercounting.
2. **Translated pages are working** (per the fresh GSC sync — earlier "all locales identical" data was a stale-cache artifact). `/es/breathing-visualizer` is at pos 6.1 with 6% CTR; `/pt/breathing-visualizer` pos 4.4; `/ja/4-7-8-breathing-timer` pulling 5 clicks. They're not big yet (2–5 clicks/page) but they're indexed and visible. Ahrefs flagged some `(Lost)` translated pages, but that's the US-only Ahrefs slice — GSC shows real activity.
3. **Several pages have fallen out of Ahrefs' top 100**: pursed-lip, breath-of-fire, anxiety/navy-seal, kids, lung-capacity, breathing-app, coherent-breathing-app, 1-minute-breathing-exercise. Likely the same impression collapse hitting tail queries hardest.
4. **DR continues to collapse** — now **4.3** (was 9 in Nov 2025). WordPress→Next.js link decay isn't done bleeding out. Link-building remains the #1 lever and isn't moving.
5. **Top engine is /4-7-8-breathing-timer**: 110 clicks / 2,368 imp / 4.6% CTR / pos 9.5 (full 90d, after fresh sync) — the template that works.
6. **/breathing-visualizer is quietly the new winner**: 80 clicks / 1,695 imp / 4.7% CTR / pos 5.3. Best CTR-at-position on the site. April-May was its strongest period (30 clicks at 5.7% CTR despite the site-wide impression drop).
7. **Biggest CTR bleed**: /breathe/coherent is at pos 7.1 with 8,347 impressions but only 0.04% CTR (3 clicks across 90d). Title/snippet rewrite is the highest-leverage micro-fix in the report.
8. **Best untapped opportunity**: "wim hof breathing" — head term **22K vol, KD 0**. We have /breathe/wim-hof but only rank pos 6 for the long-tail "wim hof breathing 3 rounds" (vol 10).
9. **Data infrastructure**: GSC re-authed today, sync resumed (5-week gap caught up). Bing OAuth still broken — token-exchange endpoint on the Railway backend is failing for fresh auth flows, not just refresh. **Needs backend fix**, not user re-auth.
