# Outreach CRM — link building & placements

Tracks every outreach target and its state, so we never double-contact and never lose a thread. Built 2026-07-19 from a 5-agent verification workflow (every row's page was fetched live) + Ahrefs competitor backlink pulls.

**Status lifecycle:** 🔲 not contacted → 📧 contacted (add date) → 💬 replied → ✅ linked → ❌ declined/dead. Update the row in the same commit as the action. When the first outreach batch ships, log it as an experiment in [SEO-EXPERIMENTS.md](SEO-EXPERIMENTS.md) with baseline referring-domain counts and pre-committed criteria.

**Rules of engagement (from the research — read before pitching):**
- Never pay for placement. Skip every "expedite"/"sponsored" upsell (Launching Next $99, SmolLaunch $99 blast, Mindful Suite $150/mo tier, findly.tools $9 is the one acceptable micro-fee if the badge is unwanted).
- Do not pitch printable cards to people who sell card decks (Coping Skills for Kids, Counselor Keri, Responsive Counselor). Pitch the free tool only.
- Source of Sources: reply only to exact-fit queries — off-topic replies get permanent removal.
- Cool Tools requires 6+ months of genuine named-user experience; founder self-submission violates their spirit. Route: ask a real long-term user.
- The recurring hook that works: **dead-app rot.** Breathe2Relax, Tactical Breather, Stop Breathe & Think/MyLife, Pacifica, and Momentous apps are discontinued but still linked on many resource pages. Pitch: "free browser tool that can't get pulled from an app store, works on school Chromebooks, no signup, has Spanish."
- Anxiety Canada ceased operations April 2026 — do not add it to any list.

## Competitor backlink intel (Ahrefs, pulled 2026-07-19)

The strategic finding: **neither ranking competitor has legitimate authority.**

- **breathwork.tools** (DR 8, 287 ref domains): ~95% purchased spam (.shop/.agency link farms). Real links: pixelthoughts.co (DR 52 meditation tool site — outreach candidate), a Hacker News appearance (hntoplinks mirror → a Show HN worked for them).
- **box-breathing.org** (308 ref domains): the impressive-looking .edu links (niu.edu DR 81, wesleyan, evergreen, dickinson, deusto.es) are ALL nofollow blog-comment spam dropped on recipe/craft/chess posts in a March 2026 campaign, plus the same SEOExpress spam farms. Their only legit links: AppRater (dofollow), Woy.ai, a Product Hunt launch (Nov 2025).
- Implication 1: the SERPs we're losing are NOT protected by real authority — a handful of genuine links from this CRM plus content quality can compete. The "DR 0.2 is the ceiling" narrative overweights competitors' fake DR.
- Implication 2: do not copy their tactics (comment spam, purchased links) — both profiles are one Google spam-update away from collapse.
- Not yet pulled: rrrelax.app, breathub.com (same pattern expected; check if a specific SERP contradicts).
- Extra candidate from the pull: **pixelthoughts.co** — real DR 52 calm-tool site that links free tools; find their links page/contact. Status: 🔲
