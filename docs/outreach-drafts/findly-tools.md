A breathing tool would be an outlier here, which cuts both ways: less competition, but also fewer relevant readers.

A directory that markets its links as an SEO product is closer to the paid-placement line than the CRM row implies.

- **Recommendation:** submit on the genuinely free tier only. Do not buy the $79 SEO Growth Package, the $19 tier, or a sponsor spot. The CRM allows a $9 badge-removal micro-fee as Abi's call; the packages that are explicitly sold as backlinks are a different thing and should be declined on the same logic that rules out the competitors' purchased links.
