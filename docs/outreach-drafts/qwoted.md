**Expertise areas:** paced breathing and breathwork technique; stress and panic self-management; free and low-friction digital mental-health tools; accessibility of wellness tools on managed school and workplace devices.

**Bio:**

I build deepbreathingexercises.com, a free browser tool for paced breathing used by people who want a technique to follow rather than an app to install. I work on the practical end of this: which breathing patterns people actually finish, why "take a deep breath" advice often makes panic worse, and why so many of the breathing apps that public resource pages recommend no longer exist. I can speak to technique mechanics, to what the evidence does and does not support, and to the state of free mental-health tooling.

### Pitch discipline

1. Free tier has a monthly pitch cap. Spend it only on exact fits: breathing technique, stress or panic self-management, free wellness tools, or digital tools on locked-down devices.
2. Do not pitch on general meditation, sleep, or wellness-industry business stories. Those are not our beat and burn the cap.
3. Lead every pitch with the substantive claim. The product is context, not the argument.
4. Keep claims defensible. Slow paced breathing increasing vagally mediated HRV during practice is supportable. Treating anxiety is not.
