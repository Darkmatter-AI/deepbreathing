### Standing rules for this channel

1. Answer the question first. The link goes in the signature, not the argument.
2. Never send a reply that would still make sense if you swapped in a different query. That is the definition of off-topic here.
3. Do not make clinical claims. Paced breathing raising vagally mediated HRV during practice is defensible. Curing anxiety is not.
4. Budget roughly 30 to 60 minutes a week for triage. Most days the right action is deleting the digest.
