**Description (neutral, catalogue voice):**

Deep Breathing Exercises is a free browser-based paced-breathing tool. It runs a visual pacer through inhale, hold, exhale and hold, with five built-in patterns: box breathing (4-4-4-4), 4-7-8, coherent breathing (5.5-5.5), the physiological sigh, and Wim Hof rounds. Phase length is adjustable from 3 to 8 seconds and sessions run from one to five minutes. There is no download, no account and no paid tier. The interface is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese and Portuguese, and the pacer can be embedded in another page. It works on any modern browser, including managed school and work devices where app installs are blocked.
